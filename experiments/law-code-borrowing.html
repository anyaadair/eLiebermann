Which law codes borrow from which

Each arc joins two law codes that scholars link clause to clause: Liebermann's comparisons, Wormald's and Kennedy's tables, Whitelock's and Jurasinski and Oliver's notes. Codes run left to right in the order of the kings, then the anonymous texts and the Anglo-Norman law books. An arc's width is the number of clause links.
