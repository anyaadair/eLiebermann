The page numbers agree throughout.

- **Fixed on the site.**
    - *twelfhynde*: "tþuelfhindus" had thorn where the Word document has wynn (**tƿuelfhindus**), the same slip as in *spic* and *spræc*. A search of the whole dictionary for thorn after a consonant found no more; the rest are compounds such as *weofodþegn*, and "tþr" in *þriding*, where Liebermann lists the spellings of the initial sound. The README's note on wynn now includes it.
- **Where Þ begins.** The Word document agrees with the README: *tyrian* is the last T entry and *þ* the first Þ entry, both on p. 216. The README note says so.
- **To check against the print (2).** In the running list, [To check against the print](to-check-against-print.md).
- **Slips in the Word document (about 25).** The site is already right. See below.
- **Entry divisions.** In the Word document these headwords are not in bold, so they run into the entry before: *[-tægl]* and *II) tǣl* (into *[tæl]*), *teolung* and *II) teon* (into *teon*), *F toille* (into *tol*), *tyn* and *I) tynan* (into *tynan*), *I) þa* (into *ꝥ*), *þære* (into *þæræt*), *þæs* (into *þæs*), *II)–IV) þæt* (into *þæt*), *þan*, *þas*, *þassum* and *þe* (into the entries before them). ## Slips in the Word document

The site already has these right:

- **Law codes and numbers:** Hl for III (*toll*, In Cn III), 10 for 1 O (*twelfhynde*), 1) for I) (*-the(o)f*).
- **Old English:** ~sc for ~se (*Temes*), þæningum for pæningum (*twegen*).
- **Latin and French:** Praef for Præf (*Tantalus*), teuent for tenent (*terre*).
- **German misspellings:** schwa.: for schw.: (*twelfhynde*), ausgeliehien (*tunesman*), Orthographic (*þ*).
- **Greek letters:** ß for β (*to*).
- **Missing text:** a ~ in *testamentalis*; the hyphen in "-þe" (*to*).
- **Stray marks:** ) for ] in *terre* and *to*; a full stop after toslite op 3 (*toslite*).
- **Punctuation dropped** in about ten entries, such as *Tautologie* (46,1;), *teste* (3,1 ff.;), *Tosti* (II.), *travail* (28,1;).
- **Line-break hyphens left in:** Hein- rich (*Teutonici*), nach- suchen (*to*); and run together: NichtBrite (*transmarinus*).
