Where the law codes were made

The places where the kings' councils issued their laws, as the codes themselves or their editors name them: Grately, Exeter, Andover, Enham, Bath, Winchester and the rest. Each circle is a place, sized by the number of codes issued there and coloured by king. Drag the slider to watch the councils move through time; click a place for its codes.

An experiment, made from <creation><placeName> in oe-laws/. Coordinates were added by hand; identifications marked uncertain are the scholars' own doubts. Wihtbordesstan, and the letters Cnut wrote abroad, cannot be placed on the map and are listed on the right.
