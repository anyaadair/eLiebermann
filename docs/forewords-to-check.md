# Liebermann's Forewords, vol. I: readings to check against the print

The German of the Vol. I forewords (*Die Gesetze der Angelsachsen* I, 1903) was transcribed from the page images of the archive.org scan, and then collated a second time against the images by a separate reader. The points below are the readings that remain uncertain or that record a deliberate choice (for example, a misprint kept as printed). Check them against the printed book when convenient.

## Title page, dedication and Vorrede (pp. v–viii)

Page: [foreword-vol1-vorrede.html](../foreword-vol1-vorrede.html)

- page vii: the abbreviation printed in italics after 'und' is rendered 'scłł' (sc + two l's with a single crossing stroke through both); the exact glyph shape is approximated.
- page vii: Tironian nota (and/ond) printed as the figure '7'; transcribed '7' as printed.
- page vi, note 4: 'Leo' has a small mark over the e (looks like 'Léo'); checked at high zoom, it is a stray print mark — read 'Leo' (Heinrich Leo, Rectitudines singularum personarum, 1842).
- title page: 'HALLE a. S.' – the 'a.' is in smaller capitals; marked as smallcaps.
- dedication page epigraph: verified as Horace, Odes I 24, 9–10 (on the death of Quintilius); Horace reads 'Nulli flebilior quam tibi, Vergili'; Liebermann substitutes '[mihi]' in brackets, as printed. '1902' = year of Maurer's death.

## Die Überlieferung der Gesetze (pp. xv–xvii)

Page: [foreword-vol1-ueberlieferung.html](../foreword-vol1-ueberlieferung.html)

- page xv, note 2: superscript after 'II 26' read as 'a' (26<sup>a</sup>); at full magnification the tiny glyph could equally be a superscript '3'.
- page xv, note 1: the print shows a stray accent-like mark over the e of 'des' in Bémont's title; transcribed 'des' (the actual title is 'Chartes des libertés').

## Die Angelsächsischen Gesetze in Vergessenheit (pp. xliii–xliv)

Page: [foreword-vol1-vergessenheit.html](../foreword-vol1-vergessenheit.html)

- page xliv, note 1: 'Æelfred's' is printed so (sic, Æ + e; confirmed on the image), presumably a misprint for 'Ælfred's'; kept as printed in both German and English.
- page xliii/xliv: the page break falls inside the hyphenated word 'Bry-|thonischen'; the [xliv] marker is placed after the whole word 'Brythonischen' so as not to split it.

## Bearbeitung der Gesetze, pp. xlv–xlix

Page: [foreword-vol1-bearbeitung.html](../foreword-vol1-bearbeitung.html)

- page xlv, note 1: 'sketch' printed letters look like 'sketeh' at this size (c/e indistinguishable); transcribed 'sketch'.
- page xlv, note 13: Greek title printed 'Ἀρχαιονομια'; accents/breathing barely visible, given without accent on the final iota.
- page xlvi, note 19: print reads 'Loudon 1628' (misprint for London); kept 'Loudon' in German, 'London' in English.
- page xlvi, note 27: print reads 'Somuer' (misprint for Somner); kept in German, 'Somner' in English.
- page xlv: 'No(w)el' appears faintly letter-spaced; marked as gesperrt.
- page xlvii, note 2: 'ri' printed in (bold?) italic; given as italic.
- page xlvii: 'BDEHK1' is printed as a letter-spaced run of sigla with roughly even gaps (K–1 gap no wider than between the letters); kept joined as 'BDEHK1'. Elsewhere (p. xlvi) 'K 1' with a thin space is kept as printed.

## Bearbeitung der Gesetze, pp. xlix–liii

Page: [foreword-vol1-bearbeitung.html](../foreword-vol1-bearbeitung.html)

- page l, col. b: 'Rask's' appears letter-spaced in the print and is now marked sperr; spacing is slight
- page li, col. b: 'E. Thomson' - a small mark over the h; read as plain 'Thomson' (printing debris)
- page lii, col. a: stray mark after 'Et.' and note ref 11 ('Et.11' —'); taken as printing debris
- page l, note 16*: printed as '16*' (an inserted note); id given as l-16a
- letter-spacing: Twysden's, W. B. Rye (xlix n. 4) and L. G. W. Legg look unspaced in the print and are left unmarked; 'Petrie and Sharpe' is spaced throughout, including 'and'
