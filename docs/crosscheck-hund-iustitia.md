## Slips in the Word document

The site already has these right:

- **Law codes and numbers:** III As for VI As (*inceptum*), II As for IV As (*indecentia*), Wi for Wl (*iloc*), 59,l (*hundwega*), la for 1a (*hw*), 13.1 and 15. 1 for commas (*hwelc*, *huslgenga*).
- **Latin:** instramentum for instrumentum (headword, *instrumentum*), obl for abl (*iusiurando*).
- **German misspellings:** Hunderverbände (*hynden*), ersetz for ersetzt (*hwa*), tätlich for tötlich (*infirmari*), Variirt for variirt (*Indicatif*), Verhezung (*invultuatio*), ubr. for übr. (*huntað*), Frank. for Fränk. (*inservire*), de for des (*Icenild*).
- **Old English:** yrfnmen (*[ie]rfenuma*), Ironside for Irenside (*isen*).
- **Greek letters and signs:** ß for β (*hundred*, *in*, *Indicativ*, *Instrumentalis*); ♱ for † (*Innocentius*, *Iohannes*).
- **Missing text:** a ~ in *hundtwelftig* and *husl*; "= ne" (*il*).
- **Stray marks:** an opening bracket before ~ in *hwilum*; ) for ] in *intertiare*; commas after norð~ (*inne*) and V Atr (*into*); full stops after 3a (*Infinitiv*).
- **Punctuation dropped** in about fifteen entries, such as *huru* (9;), *hwa* (49,9;), *i[e]rnan* (3:), *il* (30,1; 28,2; 21,1;), *Irenside,*, *invitare* (überlassen,).
- **Line-break hyphens left in:** scil- linge (*hundtwentig*), Ik~d- stret (*Icenild*), Ge- samtinstitution (*iglise*); and run together: HundertverbandsVorsteher (*hyndenman*).
