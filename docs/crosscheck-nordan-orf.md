## Slips in the Word document

The site already has these right:

- **Law codes and numbers:** E1 for El and 1 for I (*on*, *ordinare*).
- **Old English:** ælre for selre (*on*, II) 1)), onenawan, morþweorc, Ðunres- for Þunres- (*on*), andetlan (*ondettan*), præcan for ~præcan (*onsprecan*), ceapnnge for ceapunge (*orceapunga*; the README records this as broken type in the print).
- **German misspellings:** Frank. for Fränk. (*nutricatura*), Stande for stande (*ofer*), Müsse for Musse (*on*), vom for vorn (*onforan*).
- **Greek letters and signs:** ß for β (*Numerus*, *on*); ♱ for † (*Offan*).
- **Missing text:** = in "I As 5 (VI 8,4) = super overhyrnessam" (*oferhiernes*); the comma in "ofereacan, ac,"; "on-" in *on* (Praefix) 5).
- **Stray marks:** an extra "on" in "I Cn on 2,1" (*on*); extra bars \| in *ofer*, *oferhabban* and *on*; ) for ] in *onginnan* and *ores*.
- **Punctuation dropped** in about a dozen entries, such as *nunne* (185*.), *nytan* (ptt 1:), *occisio* (Schlachtwaare,), *ofworpen* (~,), *onnime,*, *onsacan* (46,1;).
- **Line-break hyphens left in:** Nor- wyci (*Norðrigena*), for- seo (*ofer-*), East- englan (*on*), frei- willig (*on*).
