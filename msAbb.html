Manuscripts

The sigla Liebermann uses for the manuscripts and early printed texts of the laws (A for MS Harley 55, H for the Textus Roffensis…), with his German description, the manuscript's library and shelfmark, his dating, the laws it contains and his notes on it. Entries link here when they cite a manuscript.

The list follows Liebermann's Verzeichniss der einzelnen Handschriften and Fundort der Handschriften (Gesetze I, pp. xiii–xiv and xviii–xlii). Lower-case sigla (hb, lq…) are lost manuscripts that Liebermann reconstructs from their surviving copies; “not seen by Liebermann” marks the few he knew only from others. Libraries are given by their present names (the British Museum is now the British Library, the Public Record Office The National Archives); private collections are as Liebermann knew them in 1903.
