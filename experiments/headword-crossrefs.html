Liebermann's cross-references between headwords

Every s. (see), vgl. (compare) and Der. (derivatives and compounds) in the Wörterbuch, as a network of word families. Search a headword, or pick one of the largest families on the right; click any word to move to it, double-click to open its entry. Arrows point from the entry that makes the reference. Liebermann's notes on spellings (h, th, -e) and grammar link to hundreds of words each, so they are left out unless you tick them in.
