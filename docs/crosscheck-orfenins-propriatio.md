## Slips in the Word document

- **Law codes and numbers:** II Ew for I Ew (*port*: I Ew 1 is about trading "butan porte"), Cii for Cn (*primarii*), 1 for 1,1 (*oðstande*), 12. 3 for 12,3 (*pretium*).

- **Stray marks:** ) for ] in *Particip*, *persona*; extra bars \| in *par*, *probare*; Q,: (*praestare*); (cpa\| for (cpa), (*pp*).
