## Slips in the Word document

The site already has these right:

- **Law codes and numbers:** Hu for Hn (*vel*), e) for c) (*w*), 7x20 for 7×20 (*vint*).
- **Old English:** þyt for pyt (*untynan*), ligene for ~ligene (*ungel[ie]gen*), ciricþingc (*utige*), ofew~ (*wealdan*), -again for -agian (*uthlah*).
