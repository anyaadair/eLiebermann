Bulk import from Word

Choose a Word document of Wörterbuch entries, like the one the team transcribes into. This page splits it into entries, makes a TEI file for each, and gives you a zip of the files to add to the site. Everything happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

1. Choose the document

How the document is read
  • Each entry is one paragraph that starts with its headword in bold. A superscript L or F before it marks Latin or French; a headword in bold italic is a grammatical term.
  • A paragraph that doesn't start with a bold headword carries on the entry before it (entries that run over a page or column).
  • A paragraph with only a number is the page number of the printed book, and the line after it is the running head. Both are used for the page numbers, not copied into entries.
  • Law codes and abbreviations are linked from the law code list and the grammatical abbreviations.

The full rules are in docs/IMPORT.md.

2. Check the entries

Entries marked Check may need a look before they go on the site. Click a headword to preview it.
