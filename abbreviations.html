Guides

Liebermann's critical apparatus is dense. The guides and lists on this page help to expand and explain his manuscript sigla, abbreviations for law code titles, and grammatical and technical terms and abbreviations, and to identify the modern authors and works he cites. It also contains a guide to reading a dictionary entry, and English translations of Liebermann's own forewords.

How to Read a Dictionary Entry

Liebermann's own explanation of his dictionary, in English, with a worked example and a key to his citations of the laws.

Law Codes

Af, Ine, II Cn, Leis Wl… The law codes and legal texts Liebermann cites.

Manuscripts

A, B, E, H, Ld… The sigla for the manuscripts and early printed texts the laws survive in.

Grammatical Terms and Abbreviations

pl, dt, ptt, s. d.… Liebermann's German grammatical terms and shorthand, expanded and translated into English.

Authors and Works Cited

Toller, Sievers, Birch Cart. Sax., Domesday… The works Liebermann cites other than his own Gesetze, with full citations.

Liebermann's Forewords

Vorrede, Widmung, Überlieferung… Liebermann's forewords and introductory essays, rarely translated, in his German with an English translation alongside.
