# Cross-check: wiðerwordian – Zahlwort (pp. 248–253)

The entries from *[wiðerwordian]* to *Zahlwort*, the end of the *Wörterbuch*, were checked against the team's Word transcription, *wiðerwordian - Zahlwort.docx* (14 October 2026), in the same way as [A – boc](crosscheck-A-boc.md). The document begins with the end of *wiðertihtlan*, which matches the site apart from one slip in the Word document (spätererem). With this document the whole dictionary has been cross-checked.

The page numbers agree throughout.

- **Fixed on the site.**
    - **Two missing entries.** The site had no X entries at all. The Word document has two on p. 252, *-x* (the letter x and its spellings) and *xp̄* ("für Christ-: s. Abkürzung"). They are now `x.xml` and `xp.xml`, made with the site's own code from the Word document's text, and X will appear in the dictionary's letter row. The Word document's "gesehr." in *-x* is read as geschr. Both are in the running list to be checked against the print, since the scan transcription had missed them.
    - *y*: "12) verschr. für a) -þ: s. twelfhinde" had thorn where the Word document has wynn: y miswritten for wynn, as in *twelfhinde*. It is now **-ƿ**. The README's note on wynn includes it.
- **To check against the print (4).** In the running list, [To check against the print](to-check-against-print.md).
- **Slips in the Word document (about 15).** The site is already right. See below.
- **Entry divisions.** In the Word document these headwords are not in bold, so they run into the entry before: *woe-* and *II) woh* (into *woh*), *I) [-wreca]* and *II)* (into *wrang*), *[-wuna]* and *II) wund* (into *wund*), *-ye-* and *II) yfel* (into *yfel*), *F Zahlwort* (into the Latin *Zahlwort*). The site has them as separate entries already.

## Slips in the Word document

The site already has these right:

- **Law codes and numbers:** VII for VIII (*woruld*, *wor[u]ldwitan*), Prol for Pro (*woruldlag[u]*), l for El (*wyscað*).
- **Old English and Latin:** -the for -hte (*wyrcan*), pæt for þæt (*Zahlwort*), vulnas for vulnus (*wund*).
- **German misspellings:** díese (*woruld*), alt. for ält. (*woruld*), Krautgärten (*wyr[t]tun*), gesehr. (*-x*), tuteveics (*Zahlwort*).
- **Signs:** ♱ for † (*Wulfstan*); x for × (*Zahlwort*).
- **Stray marks:** full stops for the colon and comma in *Wortstellung*; commas after widerstanden (*wiðhæfton*), Laien (*woruldmannum*) and regiertem (*ymbre[ccan]*); a bar \| in *Wudustoce*.
- **Punctuation dropped** in about ten entries, such as *wiðhæfton,*, *wlite* (dial.), *woruldhlafordas,*, *woruldmannum,*, *wrecan* (40,1 f.;), *wyrcan* (52,1;), *y* (abweich., Z.).
- **Run together:** Flurod. for Flur- od. (*Zahlwort*).
