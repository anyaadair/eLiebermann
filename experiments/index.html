eLiebermann experiments

Trial visualisations of the dictionary and the laws, made from the TEI. Not linked from the site.

  • Which law codes borrow from which

    Arcs between law codes, from the clause-to-clause comparisons of Liebermann, Wormald, Kennedy, Whitelock and Jurasinski and Oliver.

  • Law codes by shared vocabulary

    A network of law codes joined by the Wörterbuch headwords that cite both, with a headword search.

  • Liebermann's cross-references between headwords

    Word families from Liebermann's s., vgl. and Der.

  • Which manuscripts carry which law codes

    Law codes at the date they were made, joined to the manuscripts and early prints that carry them at the date they were written.

  • Subjects that appear together

    Subject keywords joined when they are tagged on the same clauses more often than chance.

  • Chains of reuse from one clause

    Follow a clause forward to the later clauses that take it up, and back to the ones it draws on.

  • Dating the law codes

    Each scholar's date for each code, side by side, to show where they agree and differ.

  • Where the law codes were made

    A map of the places where the codes were issued, with a slider through time.
