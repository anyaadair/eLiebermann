The page numbers agree throughout.

- **Fixed on the site.**
    - **Asterisks left in the text.** In *folcriht* and *fon* the site had asterisks round an italic passage, where the scan transcription marked italics with \*…\* and the marks were never converted. The Word document has the text without them. A search of the whole dictionary found the same in *lex* and *mearcgemot* (in L and M, not yet cross-checked). In all four, the asterisks are gone, the passage between them is now italic as it should be (it had been entered with the italics the wrong way round, so the German was in roman and the law codes in italic), and words split by a line break are closed up ("Rechtmässigkeit", "trennbares", "Gesetzbücher"). In *lex* this means the citations EGu 9, Ine 43,1 and seven from Hn are now linked and listed.
    - The other asterisks in the dictionary were checked and are Liebermann's own: after a citation (ECf 34,1a\*), as a superscript, or before a reconstructed form (\*oferseoness).
- **To check against the print (4).** In the running list, [To check against the print](to-check-against-print.md).
- **Slips in the Word document (about 35).** The site is already right. See below.
- **Entry divisions.** In the Word document these headwords are not in bold, so they run into the entry before: *I) fæstan* and *II) [-fæstan]* (into *fæst*), *fal[o]d* and *II) fals* (into *fals*), *feonge* (into *feondscype*), *A) for* and *B) for* (into *-for-*), *B) fore* and *C) fore* (into *fore*). ## Slips in the Word document

The site already has these right:

- **l for 1, 11 for II, WI for Wl:** *forfaire* (l)), *f[ie]rdung* (fyrdigce 11), *fidelis* (WI), *findan* (= 1 Cn).
- **German misspellings:** Hergäbe (*foregeþingie*), fehlte for fehlt (*forene*), Viertel-Bauer and Firm-Patenkind run together (*f[eo]rðing*, *filiolus*), Staatsbürger-Versammlung run together (*folc*).
- **Greek letters:** ß for β (*fon*, *forgieldan*).
- **Missing text:** a dative ~ (*folgere*); the bars \| in *feorða* and *feowertig*.
- **Stray marks:** an opening bracket before ~ in *fæsten*; [ for ( in *fædrenmægas*; ) for ] in *fiscalinus*; a hyphen in bef~-, (*fleon*); colons after ECf 4 (*feudum*) and II Cn 26,1 (*findan*).
- **Numbers:** 23. 1 for 23,1 (*fæder*); pl: 1 for pl 1: (*forbeodan*).
- **Punctuation dropped** in about fifteen entries, such as *fæhðe* (1,2;), *feormfultume* (69,1;), *finger* (goldf~,), *folgian* (op 3:), *forberste* (53,1;), *forg[ie]fan* (op 3:).
- **Line-break hyphens left in:** forð- f~ (*faran*), ~-gelde (*forgieldan*), - gemot (*folc*).
