## Slips in the Word document

The site already has these right:

- **Numbers:** 1 for I (*wita*).
- **German misspellings:** Kreig (*werra*), Walliscr (*W[ie]lisc*), Gitate (*willan*), Reckten (*wite*).
- **Greek letters and signs:** ß for β (*wita*); ♱ for † (*Wilhelm*, *Wintanceaster*).
- **Brackets:** ) for ] in *we[rw]ulf*, *Wilhelm* and *willan*; a missing [ before s. in *wer*; a misplaced ' in "(Staatslast')]" (*werian*).
- **Stray marks:** pl' for pl (*weddian*); a bar \| in *weorc*; a comma after orwige (*wige*).
- **Punctuation dropped** in about a dozen entries, such as *weorc* (19,1;), *were* (8,1;), *w[ie]rnan* (pl 3:), *wierðe* (8,1;), *willan* (21;), *witan* (op 2:, 3:, pl 2:).
- **Run together:** KönigsWergeld (*wergield*).
