Law Codes

Law codes and legal texts as Liebermann abbreviates them, with their German expansions and English names. Entries link here when they cite a law code. The manuscripts have a list of their own.

The German expansion and the start page are as Liebermann prints them in his list of abbreviations (“Alphabetisches Verzeichniss der Citirzeichen”, Gesetze I, p. xi), with his ditto marks written out; a few abbreviations used in the dictionary that are not in that list say so. English gives the names editors use (Attenborough, Robertson, Whitelock, Oliver, Jurasinski and Oliver, Rabin), with the editor named where the names differ. Everything else (manuscripts, editions, where a code was issued) is under Manuscripts, editions and notes.
