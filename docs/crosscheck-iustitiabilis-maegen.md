# Cross-check: iustitiabilis – mægen (pp. 128–137)

The entries from *iustitiabilis* to the start of *mægen* were checked against the team's Word transcription, *iustitiabilis - mægen.docx* (13 October 2026), in the same way as [A – boc](crosscheck-A-boc.md). The document begins with the end of *iustitia*, which matches the site apart from slips in the Word document (comitates, – for =), runs through K, and ends partway through *mægen*.

## Slips in the Word document

The site already has these right:

- **Law codes and numbers:** Em for Hn (*iustitiabilis*), III Atr for VI Atr (*lagu*), II for 11 (*landbygene*), 1 for I (*lagu*, *land*, *lufian*), I7 for 17 (*Lundoniensis*), 7,21 for 72,1 (*lif*), pt for ptt (*læfan*).
- **German misspellings:** díese (*land*), Staatsbürgerl. for staatsbürgerl. (*landcop*), leben for Leben (*leger*), Untertan for untertan (*ligius*), war' for wär' (*lytel*), Amtern for Ämtern (*macian*).
- **Old English and Latin:** lihtingc (*li[e]hting*), terrae for terræ (*landleod*).
- **Stray marks:** an opening bracket before ~ in *land*; a hyphen in utl~-, (*lædan*) and [Mittel]-finger (*lung*); a full stop after ~ (*læsseþegen*); a doubled bracket in *landbygene*; ) for ] in *latro*; a colon after Atr (*land*).
- **Missing text:** the ~ after Englal and geneatl (*land*); the colon in "für l:" (*ll*).
- **Punctuation dropped** in about fifteen entries, such as *ladrinc* (79,6;), *lædan* (übr.);), *landmann* (7;), *langeran* (367*,), *lesan* (A;), *liflyre* (Lebens, Sterben).
- **Line-break hyphens left in:** bewirt- schaftete (*land*), Ge- setzbücher (*lex*).
