Law codes by shared vocabulary

Two law codes are joined when many of the same Wörterbuch headwords cite clauses in both. Each code keeps only its closest neighbours (by the Jaccard overlap of their word lists), so the clusters show families of texts that use the same words. Search a headword to light up every code it is cited in; click a code for its closest codes and its most distinctive words.

An experiment, made from the law code citations (<xr type="lawCode">) in entries/. Codes with fewer than three cited words are left out.
