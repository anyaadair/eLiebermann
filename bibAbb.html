Authors and Works Cited

The works Liebermann cites in the Wörterbuch other than his own Gesetze, with the full citation of each. Citations of these texts in eLiebermann entries link here.

Liebermann did not print a full bibliography for the Wörterbuch or for the Gesetze as a whole. In his dictionary entries and notes, he cites by author and a short title, often abbreviated in several ways (“Birch Cart. Sax.”, “Birch Cartul.”). He does give two short lists of works in the Gesetze: the abbreviations of the Sachglossar (II.2, p. 262) and the key to the earlier editions of each law (I, p. LIV). Every work in those lists is included here, and the note quotes Liebermann’s own entry. Full citations here are reconstructed from Liebermann’s short titles and the pages and dates he gives. Where he does not say which edition he used, the one current when the Wörterbuch appeared (1906) is given, and the note says so. Citations that are uncertain are marked to check.

Under construction The full citations have not yet been checked one by one against the books themselves; journal articles in particular are given as Liebermann cites them, by journal, volume, year and page.
