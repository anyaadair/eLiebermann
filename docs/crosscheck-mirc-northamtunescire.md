## Slips in the Word document

The site already has these right:

- **Law codes and numbers:** WI for Wl (*nent*), Quadrm for Quadr (*murdre*), 5.1, 8.2 and 3. 2 for commas (*mynster*, *niman*).
- **German misspellings:** Moderniserung for Modernisirung (headword), ðffentlichen (*mundbryce*), Versäumnis for versäumnis (*mortuus*), Præfixe for Praefixe (*Negation*).
- **Greek letters:** ß for β (*n*, *ne*, *nellan*).
- **Brackets:** ( for [ in *nemne* ((dreimal)); [ for | in *missenlic*; a missing [ before 5/8 (*mundbryce*).
- **Stray marks:** = and –– inserted in *mynster*; "falsch," with a misplaced comma (*miswissige*); a comma after þonne (*na*) and s. d. (*mundbryce*).
- **Punctuation dropped** in about ten entries, such as *morðslihtum,*, *mundbryce* (mund,), *nenes,*, *Nicholaes* (voc.:), *nieddæda* (25;).
- **Line-break hyphens left in:** mund- byrd (*mund*).
