- **Fixed on the site.**
    - **Page numbers.** Page 206 begins inside *swæðer*, but the 37 entries after it (*swan* to *swing[e]*) were labelled page 205. They are now page 206, with their scan links. (This was one of the pages flagged in the [first cross-check](crosscheck-A-boc.md#page-numbers-elsewhere).)
    - *spic* and *spræc*: the manuscript forms "sþic" and "sþræt" had thorn where the Word document has wynn (**sƿic**, **sƿræt**). Thorn after s is impossible in Old English, so the wynn is right. A search of the dictionary found no other "sþ" outside compounds of *þegn*. The README's note on wynn now mentions them.
