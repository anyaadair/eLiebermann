# Cross-check: A – boc (pp. 1–25)

The entries for A to *boc* were checked against the team's Word transcription, *A - boc.docx* (version of 13 October 2026). The entries on the site are Claude's transcription from the page scans, so these are two independent transcriptions of the same pages. Where they agree, both are very probably right. Where they differ, one of them has made a slip.

Of the 102:

- **Fixed on the site (4).** These were the site's slips:
    - *að*: the typing slip "Espisc 5" is now **Episc** 5, linked to the law code.
    - *abnegatio* (p. 2) and *æfw[i]erdlan* (p. 5) are entries of their own again, as in the Word document. They had been run into *abnegare* and *æfwerdelsan*.
    - **Page numbers.** Page 4 begins inside *acquietare*, but the 54 entries after it (*acquirere* to *-æ-*) were labelled page 3, so page 4 had no entries at all. They are now page 4, with their scan links.
- **Checked against the print (11).** Questions where neither reading was clearly right, answered by an editor from the book (see below). Four led to changes on the site.
- **Slips in the Word document (about 80).** The site is already right and is unchanged. They are listed below so the Word file can be corrected.
- **Entry divisions (7).** In the Word document a headword is not in bold, so its entry runs into the one before (*ærendgewrit*, *æx*, *Æxeceaster*, *afangen*, *Augustinus*, *behwyrfð*). The site has them as separate entries already. *boc* stops at "Buch VI" because the document ends there. The rest of it will be in the next document.

## Slips in the Word document

The site already has these right. Corrections to make in *A - boc.docx*:

- **l for 1:** *abbod* (VII 31,l, also VII for VIII), *acquietare* (l)), *acuman* (l020,11), *æfter* (l020,9), *Ælf[h]eah* (l)), *ahreddingge* (l020,6), *avant* (6,l), *be* (la)), *birele* (l6), *bisæces* (l2); and E1 for El in *al[i]efan* and *an*.
- **ae for æ:** headwords *aghwaer*, *ahwaer* and the word *maeht* in *ælc*, *gefaestnian* in *Alliteration*, *uncraeftum* in *belecgan*; *Æthelred* for Æðelred.
- **German misspellings:** Klugefälle (*agenda*), Verstössen (*aweorpan*), Misserstanden (*bebycgan*), scin for sein (*befon*), Fallen, höhe and alt. for Fällen, Höhe and ält. (*be*), wic for wie (*antiquus*), Stande and stände for stande (*Accusativ*, *að*), Gallound (*a[vu]rie*), zuwerden (*ægilde*), subj for Liebermann's sbj (*aler*).
- **Law codes and numbers:** Hn for Hu (*ad*), Excom II for VII (*adwæscan*), 14 for I 4 (*antiphona*), 52.2, 14.1 and 20.2 for commas (*amender*, *aveir*), F for L (*anclidia*, a Latin word in Q).
- **Missing text:** *and* leaves out "13; se þe forstele ⁊ hit sie Af El" (the eye skipped from one "Af El" to the next).
- **Stray marks:** an opening bracket with no closing one before ~ in *and*, *apostol* and *aslean*; a quotation mark before Cnuts (*Ælfgifu*); a full stop after sintquę (*-ae-*), after ong~ (*aginnan*) and after 21 (*að*); "Besitzer"?" (*beorgan*).
- **Punctuation dropped** at the end of a citation (a full stop, colon or semicolon) in about 30 entries, such as *a* (15,1.), *abrecan* (I Cn 3;), *aetfon* (op 3:), *ban* (pl:).
- **Brackets:** ( for [ in *Accusativ*, ) for ] in *Abkürzungen*, *A[u]gust* and *beran*.
