## Slips in the Word document

The site already has these right:

- **Language marker:** L for F in *colpe* (a French verb, Leis Wl).
- **l for 1, H for II:** *communicare* (14,l. XI, lb.), *concilium* (l) and Wl ep l); HCn for II Cn (*comes*).
- **German misspellings:** Eingebomer (*compatriota*), Frank. for Fränk. (*congildo*, *coniectare*), auszuspreckende (*consilium*), sehon for schon (*consul*, *consulatus*), beschulten (*culpare*), reichisunmittelbare (*cyning*), stände for stande (*comparare*), Wohlgefallen for wohlgefallen (*cweman*), consulates for consulatus (*comitatus*).
- **Greek letters:** ß for β (*comes*, *Conjugation*).
- **Missing text:** "Grafenamt" (*consulatus* 1)); "V" after Iud Dei (*Conjugation*).
- **Numbers:** 12.10 for 12,10 (*consulatus*); 13; 1 for 13,1 (*cyning*); 1: for l: in *d* (hinter l).
- **Stray marks:** an opening bracket before ~ in *comes*; a doubled closing bracket in *cwideleas*; full stops after landlagu (*consuetudo*) and aðe (*cyðan*).
- **Punctuation dropped** in about a dozen entries, such as *commarcio* (Rb),), *communis* (37,1;), *cuman* (7;), *cwicæhtum,*, *cyssan* (op 3:), and II. and I. after rulers' names (*Cuonradus*, *Curtehose*, *Conquestor*).
- **Line-break hyphens left in:** Anglo-normannen (*comes*), post-communio (*communio*), lib- ban (*Conjugation*), Herr- schaftsprivileg (*consuetudo*), ~-diendum (*custodire*).
