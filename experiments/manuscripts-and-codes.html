Which manuscripts carry which law codes

Law codes sit on the top band at the date they were made; the manuscripts and early prints that carry their text sit on the bottom band at the date they were written. Each line is a code copied in a manuscript, its width the number of clauses whose text comes from that witness. Long slanting lines are long gaps between a law and its surviving copies.
