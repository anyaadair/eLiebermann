## Slips in the Word document

The site already has these right:

- **l for 1, 1 for I:** *ceapgield* (l)), *cheminus* (l218), *clausura* (l027,6); *bocland* (1 Atr), *ca* (Leis Wl 14 1, for the siglum I), *ciricwagum* (1 Cn); and I for 1 in *brydguma* (brigguma I).
- **German misspellings:** Farhrhabe (*bry[g]dan*), fahrhabe (*bringan*), Recten (*byrdenn*), alt. for ält. (*candela*), Einkauft (*ceap*), zugefügts. (*-ch-*), Karlien (*Cherrela*), eingestchen (*cognoscere*).
- **Latin and Greek letters:** ß for β (*capitale*); headword *caruca* for *carruca*, which then gives *caruca* as a variant (*carruca*).
- **Numbers:** 12.818 for 12,818 (*cheminus*); 1f; and 2f; for 1 f.; and 2 f.; (*Britannia*).
- **Missing text:** "pl 3:" (*clipian*); "):" closing the bracket after S. 536⁷⁹ (*bur[g]gemot*).
- **Stray marks:** an opening bracket before ~ in *brycgbot* and *carcern*; full stops after fæstenb~ (*bryce*), wullc~ (*camb*), ~la (*ceorl*) and - cch (*-cch*); ( for [ in *bry[g]dan*.
- **Punctuation dropped** at the end of a headword or citation in about a dozen entries, such as *Bretone,*, *canonicas,*, *cel,*, *ciricanmannes,*, *Hrofec~,* (*ceaster*), *III)* (*buton*).

## Typography

As in A – boc, the 41 entries that differ only in typography keep the site's conventions (see the [table there](crosscheck-A-boc.md#typography)). The Word document also writes = as – in two parallel citations (*cieping*, *clænsian*). The site's = is the usual sign for a parallel passage and is kept.
