Authors and Works Cited

The works Liebermann cites in the Wörterbuch and in his notes to the laws, other than his own Gesetze, with the full citation of each. Citations of these works in eLiebermann entries and in the notes on the law pages link here.

Liebermann did not print a full bibliography for the Wörterbuch or for the Gesetze as a whole. In his dictionary entries and notes, he cites by author and a short title, often abbreviated in several ways (“Birch Cart. Sax.”, “Birch Cartul.”). He does give two short lists of works in the Gesetze: the abbreviations of the Sachglossar (II.2, p. 262) and the key to the earlier editions of each law (I, p. LIV). Every work in those lists is included here, and the note quotes Liebermann’s own entry. Full citations here are reconstructed from Liebermann’s short titles and the pages and dates he gives. Where he does not say which edition he used, the one current when he wrote is given, and the note says so: for the Wörterbuch, the edition current in 1906; for works cited only in the notes to the laws, the edition current in 1916 for the notes in vol. III, or in 1903 for those in vol. I. Where his page numbers or a mark such as “I²” point to a particular edition, that edition is given. Citations that are uncertain are marked to check.

Under construction The full citations have not yet been checked one by one against the books themselves; journal articles in particular are given as Liebermann cites them, by journal, volume, year and page.
