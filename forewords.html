Liebermann's Forewords

Liebermann opened each volume of Die Gesetze der Angelsachsen with a foreword, and set before the texts a series of short essays on how the laws were made, preserved, forgotten and studied. These texts are seldom translated. Each page here gives Liebermann's German, paragraph by paragraph, with an English translation alongside.
