A law code or abbreviation isn't linked?

Check that the spelling is in markLaw.json (law codes), manuscripts.json (manuscripts) or markforward.json (abbreviations). There may be a variant that isn't listed, such as "3.Pers" for "3. Pers". If the variant will come up again, add it to the right file; otherwise edit the XML above by hand.

Law codes are only linked in roman (upright) type, because Liebermann's italics are German: Abt is "abbot", not the laws of Æthelberht.
