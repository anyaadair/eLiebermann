# Numbered sections joined into one entry (October 2026)

Where Liebermann numbers the sections of a headword I), II), III) … (as for *þæt*), each section had been made a file of its own. They are now one entry each, as in the print. The later files are removed; `data/entry-redirects.json` sends their old addresses to the joined entry, and links to them from other entries now point at the joined entry.
